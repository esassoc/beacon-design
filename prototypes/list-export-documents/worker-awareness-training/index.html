Conduct Spill Response and Containment Training

COA 11.22

Permittee trains Project personnel who handle petroleum products and other hazardous materials in emergency response and spill containment techniques as required by the Spill Prevention, Control, and Countermeasure Plan. Training is delivered before those personnel perform the covered work and completion is captured on a signed roster filed with the SPCCP.

Train and Staff Fish Salvage Teams Before Site Access

COA 11.35

Before fish rescue workers access the work site, Permittee provides safety training and assigns at least one Designated Fisheries Biologist to each fish salvage team. Electrofishing is staffed as one or two teams of three to four people, each with a unit operator and two or three netters. The product is a dated safety-training roster and a salvage team roster for the salvage event.

Train Tugboat and Barge Operators on Habitat Impacts

COA 11.36

Permittee ensures every tugboat and barge operator working on the Project is trained to minimize impacts on Covered Species habitat, including reducing the effects of wake on vegetated banks, before that operator runs a Project vessel. Completion is documented for each named operator.
