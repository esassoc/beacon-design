Iconography

Icons are inline Lucide SVGs — same family Beacon ships, no icon dependency. The esa-icon primitive inherits currentColor and maps to five sizes (xs 14 · sm 16 · md 20 · lg 24 · xl 28).

Sizes

xs · 14sm · 16md · 20lg · 24xl · 28

Color

Icons inherit currentColor — set color on a wrapper to tint them. These use Beacon's themed semantic roles.
