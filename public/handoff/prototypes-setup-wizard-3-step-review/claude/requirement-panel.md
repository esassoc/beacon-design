# Requirement panel

The selected requirement's data: name, type, phases, timing (frequency, milestone, direction, offset, recurrence, and the words as stated), species, construction activities, seasons, responsible party, deliverable. A value that came from the text lights its words when its field is hovered. Its flag, if any, sits on top with the one-click fix. With nothing selected it lists the commitment's requirements; on uncovered text it offers New requirement or Not a requirement.

## Key decisions
- Suggested by Aldo (dashed chip) until any edit, then Confirmed (solid).
- Milestone, species and seasons pick from the project registry, so a merge changes their options.
