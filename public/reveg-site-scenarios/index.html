Source: Reveg_Mitigation_Balance_20261111_TG-CF.xlsx, Sheet1, green columns. Credits = Final Total Reveg Credits (col V). Allocated = Total Credits Allocated as of 2026 NPW (col Y), or Credits Allocated as of 2025 ASR (col W) when that basis is selected. Unused = credits − allocated (matches col Z). Acres = sq ft ÷ 43,560.
Watershed balance = unused credit at kept sites − credits allocated to dropped sites. Red means the balance is negative and the dropped allocations cannot be covered inside the watershed. Credit from dropped sites is moved to kept sites with the most unused credit first, preferring sites that meet all success criteria.
Basins: North County (Santa Clara) covers Coyote, Guadalupe, Lower Peninsula and West Valley; South County (Pajaro) covers Uvas/Llagas. Basin and all-basin figures add up the positive watershed balances as excess credit and list any watershed shortfalls separately, because credit is not moved between watersheds.
Success criteria (cols F–K): tree survivorship (TS) >75%, native cover (NC) >75% below OHW or >50% above OHW, non-native cover (NNC) <10%. Green meets the threshold. Yellow misses it by 10 percentage points or less (TS 65–75%, NC 40–50% or 65–75%, NNC 10–20%). Red misses it by more than 10 points. TS shows n/a for sites with no tree credits. Tree credits (col L) are shown for reference and are not balanced.
